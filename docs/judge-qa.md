# Judge Q&A

## Does CareHub AI replace nurses?

No. It supports nurses by prioritizing early-warning alerts. Nurses still make final care decisions.

## Is this a diagnostic device?

No. The prototype detects risk patterns and provides early-warning support. It does not diagnose pressure ulcers or other medical conditions.

## Why not build a full smart bed?

A full smart bed is expensive and hard to deploy. CareHub AI is modular, so it can attach to existing beds and IV stands.

## What does AI do?

AI-style logic classifies pressure and IV risk patterns, ranks urgency, and explains recommended nurse actions.
