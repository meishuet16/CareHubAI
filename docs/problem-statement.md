# Problem Statement

In hospitals and elderly care centres, nurses and caregivers often monitor several patients at once. Many bedside risks develop gradually between scheduled manual checks.

Two common examples are prolonged pressure and IV drip issues. A bedridden or wheelchair-bound patient may remain in one pressure position for too long, increasing pressure-ulcer risk. An IV drip may also become nearly empty or flow abnormally before the next manual round.

CareHub AI addresses this gap by continuously monitoring selected bedside signals and converting them into prioritized, explainable nurse alerts.
