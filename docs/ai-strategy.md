# AI Integration Strategy

CareHub AI uses AI at the decision-support layer. The system does not make medical diagnoses.

The AI-style risk engine analyzes:

- Pressure intensity.
- Pressure duration.
- Pressure zone distribution.
- IV remaining time.
- IV flow abnormality.
- Number of simultaneous risks.

The output is a priority score, a risk level, a recommended action, and a short explanation for nurses.
