# CareHub AI Proposal Summary

CareHub AI is an AI-assisted smart bedside nursing assistant for Track 1: Healthcare Technology.

The system is designed as a modular bedside add-on rather than a full smart hospital bed. It monitors pressure-ulcer risk and IV drip status, then ranks nurse alerts through a central dashboard.

The MVP includes:

- A 4-zone pressure monitoring model.
- IV drip status estimation.
- A multi-bed nurse dashboard.
- AI-style risk scoring and alert priority ranking.

CareHub AI supports nurses and caregivers. It does not diagnose medical conditions, replace clinical judgement, or control real IV flow.
